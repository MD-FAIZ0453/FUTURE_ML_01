Prophet Algorithm Explanation

What is Facebook Prophet?

Prophet is an open-source forecasting library developed by Meta's Data Science team. It is optimized for business time-series data which frequently display strong seasonal components and multiple years of historical records.

How does it model time series?

Prophet uses an additive model where non-linear trends are fit with yearly, weekly, and daily seasonality, plus holiday effects. It is expressed mathematically as:

y(t) = g(t) + s(t) + h(t) + εt
  • g(t): piecewise linear or logistic growth trend
  • s(t): periodic changes (yearly/weekly seasonality)
  • h(t): holiday effects (impact of specific days)
  • εt: error term (unexplained variance)
Why choose Prophet? It handles missing values, large outliers, and dramatic structural breaks (like product launches or holiday surges) extremely robustly compared to traditional ARIMA/SARIMAX models.
